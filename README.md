# Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes sales data using Python and Power BI to understand business performance, profitability, and sales trends.

## Tools Used

* Python
* pandas
* Matplotlib
* Power BI

The project includes:

* Data cleaning
* Sales analysis
* Profit analysis
* Profit margin analysis
* Regional analysis
* Product and category analysis
* Customer segment analysis
* Monthly sales trends
* Data visualization

## Key Goal

The goal of this project is to turn raw sales data into useful business insights and present the results through Python analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

### Data Preparation
The dataset was inspected for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent text values, and incorrect data types. Duplicate rows were removed, city and category values were standardized, and the order date was converted to datetime format.

Additional features were created, including:
- Profit Margin
- Order Year
- Order Month
- Order Month Name

### Key Findings
- The West generated the highest sales, while the East generated the highest total profit.
- Higher discounts were associated with lower profit margins, with a correlation of approximately -0.30.
- Electronics generated the highest total sales and profit among product categories.
- Accessories had the highest average transaction profit margin.
- Online was the strongest sales channel by total sales and profit.
- Consumer was the highest-value customer segment by total sales and profit.
- Sales and profit were strongest during the first half of the year, with May being the strongest month.
- Laptop generated the highest total product profit.
- Mouse had the highest average transaction profit margin.

### Business Recommendations
- Review discounting strategies, particularly in the West region, to determine whether margins can be improved without significantly reducing sales.
- Continue investing in the Online sales channel because it generates the highest total sales and profit.
- Maintain strong support for high-volume products such as Laptop while using high-margin accessories for cross-selling and product bundles.
